What Are Our Main Advantages
01

Tobacco heritage

  • For more than 80 years, Moldova was the main tobacco and cigarette producer for the 15 Soviet countries, delivering authentic blends and unique tastes for the whole region.
  • Moldova has a strong culture of tobacco growing and processing, with the oldest factories dating back to beginning of the last century. Moldova is also home to a rich diversity of tobacco varieties thanks to its mild climate, and highly fertile soil.

Integrated solutions

The team at International Tobacco provides full-assistance, in market research, and brand creation, thus offering an integrated solution, and a product that fulfills the necessities of the customer in a specific market.

Professional team

  • Our strength are our people, as we consistently invest in their education and development.
  • The team at International Tobacco combine cultural methods of tobacco blending, with newest technology of tobacco processing, in order to supply high quality products.
  • International Tobacco uses high quality tobacco from Moldova, Brazil, Italy, and India, thus offering unique taste, aroma and unforgettable smoke experience.

SINCE 1962

International Tobacco Company, previously known as Orhei Tobacco Factory, was established in 1962, and quickly became one of the largest supplier of processed tobacco leaf in the region, providing authentic and high quality blends to the major cigarette producer around the whole Soviet Union.

Modernization

In 2005, with the establishment of International Tobacco, huge modernization of the old factory started. The first cigarette producing machines were installed, and the creation of first brands for the local market was initiated.

Flexibility

In 2015, our primary enlarged its operations, offering flexibility and independence of the cigarette manufacturing process of outside suppliers of tobacco blends.

Integrated service

Now, we are a solid organization capable of offering a fully integrated service and product, designed solely to the preference of our customers.
