Flow pack
Flow pack

A popular choice for food packaging, flow packs enclose the product in a continuous roll of film, securely sealed on three sides.

Benefits

  • Provides excellent product visibility
  • Provides protection against contaminants
  • Barriers for gases and moisture
  • Cost-effective and efficient for higher-volume productions

Use cases

  • Food: Dried goods (e.g. rice and pasta), frozen foods, baked goods, and snacks

Retort pouch
Retort pouch

Also known as retortable or stand-up pouches, retort packaging offers a compelling alternative to industrial canning. Metal-plastic laminate pouches are designed to withstand thermal sterilization processes, ensuring product preservation and extended shelf life.

Benefits

  • Lightweight alternative to cans or bottles
  • Resealable for maximal preservation
  • Flat, gusseted base provides high product visibility and convenience to consumers
  • Efficient use of energy and materials

Use cases

  • Food: Olive oil, snacks, coffee, pet food, and ready-to-eat foods

FAQs

What is rotogravure printing?

Rotogravure printing is an industrial printing process that utilises engraved cylinders to transfer ink onto flexible packaging materials.

It offers high print quality, excellent colour saturation, and is ideal for large-scale production.

Is rotogravure right for my product?

If you are a producer of packaged goods, rotogravure printing ensures sharp, vibrant, and consistent printing results with the greatest value for your investment.

It will enhance the visual appeal of your packaging, reinforce your brand identity, and create a lasting impression on your consumers.
