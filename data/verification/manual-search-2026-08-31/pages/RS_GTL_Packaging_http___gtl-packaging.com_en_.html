ABOUT THE COMPANY

The GTL-Packaging company designs and manufactures packaging equipment, filling and dosing systems for different types of products, automatic machines for PET containers production and also complex lines for filling and packaging of the product.

MAINTAINENCE AND SERVICES

The GTL-Packaging company provides engineering services, installation of the equipment, warranty and afterwarranty maintenance,customer’s staff education, supply of spare parts and consumables and also upgrade of the existing equipment.

OUR CUSTOMERS

The GTL-Packaging equipment is used at food industry enterprises in Europe and CIS countries. The GTL-Packaging solutions for dairy industry and filling of the beverages are the most popular at the market.

THE COMPANY MARKETS

The GTL-Packaging company works at the markets of West and East Europe, Russia and CIS countries. In 2012 for Russian customers there was opened a representative office and authorized service and support departments in Moscow and St. Petersburg.

WHERE WE ARE AT

The head office and production facility of the GTL-Packaging company are situated in Kragujevac in central Serbia, it is the region with historically developed metalworking industry.

EQUIPMENT

TRIBLOKS

Container washing, product filling and capping – 3 procedures in our machine.

LABELLING MACHINES

Labelling machines of GTL-LabelPak series for applying labels of different type onto PET and glass containers.

GROUP PACKAGING LINES

Lines for single and group packaging into thermo shrinking film based on GTL-Contiterm machine

WASHING MACHINES

Modular blocks for washing and disinfection of containers before the product filling

FILLING MACHINES

Modular blocks and automatic machines for product filling into glass or PET containers.

AUTOMATIC CAPPING MACHINES

Modular blocks and automatic machines for capping of containers with the caps of different types for glass and PET bottles and cans.

PET BLOW MOULDING MACHINES

The GTL-CONTI automatic machines for production of PET containers by a method of plastic preforms blowing

PALLETIZERS AND DEPALLETIZERS

Systems of removal and alignment on the pallet for filling and packaging lines.

ADDITIONAL EQUIPMENT

Conveyers, inspection machines, weight check systems, station of condensate deleting, CIP stations, printers and others.

COMPLEX SOLUTIONS

FILLING LINES INTO BOTTLE

Complex solutions of GTL-PACKAGING for filling of liquid and viscous food products into plastic or glass container

GROUP PACKAGING LINES

Lines for single and group packaging of the product into thermo shrinking film on the base of the GTL-Contiterm automatic machines

FILLING LINES INTO CUP

Solutions for filling of wide range of liquid and viscous food products into plastic or lamister package and further transfer of the product into the cardboard tray.

SOLUTIONS FOR YOUR PRODUCT

Water and still liquids, dairy products, carbonated drinks, special viscous products – valves, dosing systems and capping of the containers.

HYGIENIC EQUIPMENT DESIGN

The systems providing purity of the automatic lines depending on requirements of the filling product
