    GRAFOBAL.
    European manufacturer of world packaging

Production of  cardboard packaging and other printed products.

GRAFOBAL, akciová spoločnosť based in Skalica, is one of the market leaders in Central and Eastern Europe with its 119-year tradition in the production of packaging and other printed  products. Together with its subsidiaries, it forms the packaging division of the Grafobal Group, a. s.

Grafobal is a specialist in the production of offset printed packaging from  cardboard, corrugated laminated cardboard and other printed products. The biggest currency is high-quality printing, a wide range of refining technologies and the innovative potential of our specialists.

OUR OFFER

Cardboard packaging

Cardboard packaging is a core product of the GRAFOBAL product range. It is used in consumer packaging: food and beverages medicinal products cosmetics tobacco products toys glass and electronics other consumer goods Processing by a special surface treatment such as relief embossing, acrylic paint printing, hot foil embossing, windowing and UV varnishing is often used.

learn more

Laminated corrugated cardboard packaging

Packaging from laminated corrugated cardboard are designated for consumer and group packaging of fragile and larger products. In the process of the production of corrugated cardboard, offset printed smooth cardboard is laminated into double-ply corrugated cardboard. The resulting material, laminated corrugated cardboard, is characterized by high-quality offset printing typical for smooth cardboard and at the same time by the strength and damping characteristics typical of corrugated cardboard.

learn more

Cartonnage

We offer the transport packaging from 3 and 5 layer corrugated cardboard in a brown or white surface design. The maximum cross-sectional dimension for which we are able to print an up to 2-color flexo printing is 1405 x 2810 mm. We focus on the production of lapel and cut-out boxes according to the FEFCO catalogue. These can be machine or hand glued as well as hand sewn.

learn more

Labels

Labels are most commonly used for the labeling of bottles for beer, wine, spirits, soft drinks, or as a container for chocolates or soft cigarette packs. We specialize in the production of cut-out labels of various shapes up to 300 x 300 mm and cut paper labels. Paper bags with euro cut-outs are mostly used for seeds. Bags without euro cut-outs are used primarily for handling cash and packing small items. The dimensions of bags can be: at least 65 x 55 mm, a maximum of 118 x 240 mm.
