DISTRIBUTION
Tobačna Grosist have moved in 2009 with its parent company Tobačna Ljubljana to Črnuče in new and modern warehouse-logistics center.
Tobačna Grostist cooperates with commercial chains, gas stations, kiosks and also retail stores and bars. It supplies more than 3000 retail shops and bars across Slovenia in total. Those customers have optional weekly delivery. Delivery is organised with external conctract partners.
