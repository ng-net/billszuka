Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantity is 500 units.

How quickly can I receive a price quote?

We prepare a price estimate within 24 hours after receiving the dimensions and quantities.
For a fully accurate quotation, a construction (dieline) file is required (and, if available, the artwork file).

What file formats do you accept?

We accept print-ready PDF files with a 3 mm bleed.
If needed, we can assist in preparing your files correctly for production.

Are the materials FSC® certified?

Yes. We work with FSC® certified materials, and our company holds an FSC® Chain of Custody (CoC) certification. This ensures full traceability of certified materials throughout the production process and allows the use of FSC labeling on the final product where applicable.

What types of varnishes and foils are available?

We offer full or partial UV varnishing (gloss or matte), as well as soft-touch lamination for enhanced surface finish and durability.

Hot foil stamping is available in various finishes, including gold, silver, and specialty or holographic foils upon request.

How are finished products packed?

Finished products are packed according to safe transport standards.
Palletization, carton packing, and labeling specifications are agreed individually based on the client’s logistics and delivery requirements.

Do you offer deliveries outside Latvia?

Yes. We regularly deliver across the European Union, providing organized logistics and delivery terms tailored to each project.
