Manufacturer and supplier

Darimex is a leading regional manufacturer of packaging materials for cigarette producers. When involved in production our materials guarantee best performance and runnability and when used in packaging they add great value to customers' brands.

​

We have happy customers and long-term partners all over the world and we are proud to be a reliable supplier to cigarette producers in more than 15 countries in Europe and Asia. Through careful selection of raw materials and closed production cycle we can offer professional service and very short lead times.
