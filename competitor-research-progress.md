### Pricing bands (public retail, indicative 2025–2026)

| Segment | Typical public price (EUR/PLN band) | Players |
|---------|--------------------------------------|---------|
| Manual / spring | ~€15–40 / 50–150 PLN | Top-O-Matic, cheap Rollo, CN generics |
| Electric entry | ~€50–90 / 200–400 PLN | PowerMatic 1+/2, Hawk HK-1/2, Powerfiller 1–2 |
| Electric mid | ~€90–150 | PowerMatic 3+/4, Hawk HK-3, Powerfiller 3–4 |
| Premium / auto | ~€150–250+ | PowerMatic 5 / V+, Powerfiller Orbit, full-auto CN |

*Wholesale B2B nets typically 30–50% below public; exact BILLS dealer nets not public — treat as estimate.*

### Feature comparison axes

1. Injection tech (piston vs slide; hopper; auto tube load)  
2. Tube formats (KS / 100 mm / slim)  
3. Count display / density control  
4. Service network in CEE  
5. Warranty validity with parallel import  
6. Bundle with tubes/papers/aromas  
7. Brand trust & reviews (Ceneo, Amazon.de, Trustpilot)

### Key strategic tensions

1. **Authorized exclusivity vs Allegro parallel import** — BILLS’ seal story is the main differentiator; grey stock erodes ASP and brand NPS.  
2. **Hawk dual role** — competing brand globally; if BILLS sells Hawk, portfolio hedges PowerMatic; if not, Hawk EU web dealers (eTutun.ro, DE/NL shops) steal share.  
3. **Powerfiller DE-AT strength** — weak CEE exclusive footprint today → opportunity or future entry risk.  
4. **GGT / Sanitex / Logista** — not machine brands; they decide whether PowerMatic gets shelf vs Powerfiller/OCB/private label.  
5. **M Tobacco Plovdiv** — controls tubes/papers gravity in SE Europe; machine attach-rate partner or rival OEM.
