**Date:** 2026-08-31

**Companion tracker:** [`competitor-research-progress.md`](../competitor-research-progress.md)

## 1. Executive summary

BILLS is not competing in “cigarettes.” It competes in **MYO hardware + the wholesale routes that put that hardware on shelves** across 12 CEE markets already mapped in BILLSzuka.

| Layer | What it is | Top threats |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **A. Brands** | Other electric/manual injectors | Powerfiller, Hawk-Matic, Allegro CN generics, Zorr rebadge |
| **B. Channels** | Wholesalers & retail networks | GGT (SK), Sanitex (Baltics), M Tobacco (BG), Allegro grey sellers, Logista (FR) |
| **C. Supply** | Brand origin & global masters | Fan Bao / Powermatic lineage; multi-badge EU retail |

**BILLS moat today:** exclusive PowerMatic authorization for PL + CEE + **warranty seal + local service** (explicitly refuses service/parts for non-authorized units).

**Main vulnerability:** parallel import on Allegro/Merkandi + multi-brand shops that treat PowerMatic as one SKU among Powerfiller/Rollo/Hawk/no-name.

**Strategic recommendation (short):** weaponize BILLSzuka data to (1) lock GGT/Sanitex/Veletabak-class accounts before Powerfiller enters CEE exclusive deals, (2) publish a public “authorized seller check” against grey market, (3) decide Hawk portfolio stance (carry vs fight).

- Self-describes as exclusive importer/distributor for many global tobacco brands; retail via holding partners.  
- **Implication:** Getting PowerMatic (not Powerfiller/OCB injectors) into GGT catalog is worth more than 10 small resellers.

### 5.3 Sanitex (Baltics)

- BILLSzuka INTEL already flags as **TOP strategic partner** for LT+LV+EE.  
- Competitive risk: Sanitex can equally pick a cheaper CN line or Powerfiller if margins better.

- Family manufacturer of filter tubes, papers, RYO/MYO accessories (Plovdiv hub).  
- Competes less on machines, more on **who owns the complementary basket** retailers must stock.

### 5.5 France: Logista + Royal

- Logista = structural tobacco path to buralistes.  
- Royal = accessories brands (Smoking, etc.).  
- BILLS FR catalog (21 firms in BILLSzuka) is correctly oriented at accredited suppliers, not random webshops.

**Weaknesses**

**Opportunities**
- Lock P0 wholesalers before Powerfiller CEE push

- Public “check your seller” tool fed by BILLSzuka

**Threats**
- Grey PowerMatic destroying warranty trust  
- Powerfiller exclusive deals in CZ/SK/RO  
- Sanitex/GGT choosing higher-margin private label injectors  
- Regulatory swings (DE-style dealer restrictions spreading)
